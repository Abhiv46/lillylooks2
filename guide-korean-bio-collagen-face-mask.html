Korean Bio-Collagen Face Masks: What They Are and How to Use One

The mask that fills TikTok every evening — worn for hours or overnight, and famous for turning clear as it sits. Here is what it actually is, and how to use it properly.

What is a bio-collagen face mask?

A Korean bio-collagen face mask is a hydrogel sheet mask: a soft, jelly-like sheet soaked in a skincare essence, with collagen among its headline ingredients. Collagen is a protein that is widely used in skincare products, and in K-beauty it is most often paired with hydrating ingredients in masks designed to sit on the skin for an extended wear. Unlike the thin fabric masks you might know, the hydrogel sheet is thicker, slightly cooling to the touch, and usually comes in two pieces — one for the upper face and one for the lower — so it can follow the shape of your face more closely.

How is it different from a normal sheet mask?

A standard sheet mask is worn for 10 to 20 minutes and then thrown away while the skin is still wet with essence. A bio-collagen hydrogel mask is a long-wear format: it is designed to stay on for a few hours, or overnight while you sleep. As the hours pass, the sheet gradually turns from milky white to transparent — that change is the sign the essence has been sitting against your skin, and it is the moment most people film for TikTok. The finish people talk about is a fresh, dewy look, often described on social media as “glass skin”.

How to use one, step by step

Start with a clean face: cleanse as normal and pat your skin dry. If toner or serum is part of your routine, apply it first and let it settle. Open the packet and unfold the mask — handle it gently, as hydrogel can tear if it is pulled. Place the pieces over your face, lining up the eye, nose and mouth openings, then smooth the sheet down with your fingertips so it lies flat with no air bubbles trapped underneath. Edges that lift can be pressed back down as it warms to your skin.

Then simply leave it on. Wear it for a few hours in the evening, or put it on before bed and wear it overnight. When the sheet has turned clear, peel it away, pat any remaining essence into your skin with your fingertips, and follow with your usual moisturiser if you like. Each mask is single-use, so open a fresh one next time.

When and how often?

Most people treat a mask like this as an occasional step rather than a daily one — once or twice a week is typical, often the night before an event or as part of a slower weekend routine. Because it is worn for a long stretch, many wearers prefer it on a night when they are not rushing anywhere. If your skin is on the sensitive side, do a patch test first: apply a small piece of the mask to your inner arm or jawline, wait, and check how your skin feels before committing to a full wear. Keep unopened masks stored somewhere cool and dry, out of direct sunlight.

Common mistakes to avoid

Putting the mask over heavy creams or oils can stop it sitting flat, so keep the layers underneath light. Tugging the sheet into place stretches and tears hydrogel — lift and reposition instead. And if the sheet keeps sliding, you may be wearing it too early in the evening while you are still moving around a lot; many people put it on once they are settled.
