Step by Step

How to Use

Step 1

Cleanse your face and pat it dry. Apply your usual toner or serum first if that is part of your routine.

Step 2

Open the mask, unfold it and place it over your face, smoothing it down so it sits flat with no air bubbles.

Step 3

Leave it on for a few hours, or wear it overnight — the hydrogel gradually turns clearer as it sits on the skin.

Step 4

Peel the mask off and pat any remaining essence into your skin, then follow with moisturiser if you like.

Good to Know

Questions & Answers

How long do I wear the mask?

A few hours, or overnight while you sleep. It is a long-wear hydrogel mask rather than a quick 15-minute sheet mask.

How often should I use one?

Most people use a mask like this once or twice a week, as an occasional step alongside their normal routine.

Is it suitable for sensitive skin?

It is a hydrating hydrogel sheet mask format used in many routines. If your skin is sensitive, do a patch test on a small area first and follow the guidance on the TikTok Shop listing.
