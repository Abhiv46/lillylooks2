Step by Step

How to Use

Step 1

Press a puff into your loose or pressed powder and tap off the excess.

Step 2

Press and roll the puff over the areas you want to set — the T-zone is a common starting point.

Step 3

Use a clean puff, or a clean side, for touch-ups through the day so you are not moving oil around your face.

Step 4

Wash the puffs with gentle soap, rinse them well and leave them to air dry completely before storing.

Good to Know

Questions & Answers

What are powder puffs used for?

Applying and setting powder and foundation with a soft, even finish — they are a staple for baking, setting makeup and touch-ups.

Can I use the puffs with liquid foundation?

Yes, a puff can press and blend liquid and cream products too. Many people keep separate puffs for powder and for liquids.
