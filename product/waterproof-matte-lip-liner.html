Step by Step

How to Use the Waterproof Matte Lip Liner

Step 1

Start with clean, dry lips. If you used lip balm earlier in your routine, blot it off before lining.

Step 2

Outline your natural lip line with short, light strokes, following your lip shape.

Step 3

Fill in the lips with the liner if you want a matte base for lipstick, or wear the liner on its own.

Step 4

Finish with lipstick or gloss over the top if you like, and re-outline after eating if needed.

Good to Know

Waterproof Matte Lip Liner — Questions & Answers

Can I wear lip liner without lipstick?

Yes — a matte liner can be worn alone for a defined, polished lip look, or used as a base under lipstick or gloss.

How do I remove a waterproof lip liner?

Use an oil-based or waterproof makeup remover, then cleanse as normal.

How do I apply it neatly?

Start from clean, dry lips — blot off any lip balm first — then outline your natural lip line with short, light strokes, following your lip shape.
