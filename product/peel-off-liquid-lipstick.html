Step by Step

How to Use the Peel-Off Liquid Lipstick

Step 1

Start with clean, dry lips — avoid lip balm straight beforehand, as oils stop the film from setting evenly.

Step 2

Apply an even layer over your lips, staying inside your natural lip line.

Step 3

Keep your lips apart and let the layer dry fully, until it feels completely set.

Step 4

Lift an edge at the corner of your mouth and peel the film away slowly to reveal the stain underneath.

Good to Know

Peel-Off Liquid Lipstick — Questions & Answers

What is left on my lips after I peel it off?

A lip stain — the colour stays on your lips as a tint rather than a layer of lipstick, so there is no lipstick layer sitting on top of the skin.

My peel came off patchy — what went wrong?

Patchiness usually means the layer was uneven, or it was peeled before it had fully dried. Apply a smooth, even coat and give it a little longer to set before peeling.

How do I remove the stain at the end of the day?

An oil-based cleanser or makeup remover lifts lip stains; a gentle lip scrub helps too.
