Step by Step

How to Use the Multicoloured Rhinestone Face Gem Stickers

Step 1

Start with clean, dry skin and finished makeup, keeping the areas where gems will sit free of oils and heavy creams.

Step 2

Plan your design first — popular placements are around the eyes, along the cheekbones and in the hairline.

Step 3

Peel each gem from the sheet with clean fingers or tweezers and press it gently onto the skin.

Step 4

To remove, peel the gems off slowly at the end of the day and lift any sticky residue with your usual cleanser.

Good to Know

Multicoloured Rhinestone Face Gem Stickers — Questions & Answers

Are the face gems reusable?

The stickers are self-adhesive and made for a single wear. If the gems are stored flat and clean, some wearers reuse them with cosmetic gem glue — just check the adhesive feels secure before wearing them again.

Can I wear them over makeup?

Yes — they are designed to sit on top of a finished makeup look. They stick best on skin that is dry and free of heavy oils.

How do I take them off?

Peel them away gently from the skin. Any residue lifts off with your usual cleanser or makeup remover.
