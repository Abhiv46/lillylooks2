Step by Step

How to Use the Mini Finger Puff for Foundation

Step 1

Slip the strap over your finger so the puff sits securely on your fingertip.

Step 2

Pick up a small amount of foundation or concealer and press it onto the skin in short, dabbing motions.

Step 3

Work in small areas — under the eyes, around the nose and over blemishes — where a full-size sponge is hard to control.

Step 4

Wash the puff after use with gentle soap, rinse it and leave it to air dry.

Good to Know

Mini Finger Puff for Foundation — Questions & Answers

What is a finger puff for?

Precise blending in small areas. Its fingertip size gives you control around the eyes and nose and anywhere that needs detailed work.

Can I use it dry?

It works with liquid and cream products, used dry or very slightly damp — try both and keep the finish you prefer.

How do I clean the finger puff?

Wash it after use with gentle soap, rinse it well and leave it to air dry before using it again.
