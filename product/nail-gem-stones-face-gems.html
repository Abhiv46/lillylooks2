Good to Know

Nail Gem Stones & Face Gems — Questions & Answers

Do I need glue for the gems?

Face gems can be fixed with cosmetic gem glue for extra hold on clean, dry skin. On nails, a tacky top coat or nail glue keeps the stones in place.

Can the same stones be used on nails and face?

Yes — the kit is a double-duty mix sized for both manicures and face designs, so you can match your nails to your makeup look.

How do the gems come off?

Face gems peel away gently from the skin; nail stones come away with your usual polish or gel removal routine.
