Good to Know

Dual-Sided Exfoliating Gloves for Body — Questions & Answers

Why do the gloves have two textures?

Each glove is dual-sided: one texture for gentler everyday washing and a firmer one for buffing rougher areas, so you can adjust as you go.

How often should I exfoliate?

Once or twice a week is typical for body exfoliation. If your skin feels sensitive, use the gloves less often and with a lighter touch.

Are the gloves useful before fake tan?

Yes — exfoliating in the shower is a standard prep step before applying self-tan, because it leaves an even base to work on.
