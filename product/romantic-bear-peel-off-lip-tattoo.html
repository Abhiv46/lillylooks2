Step by Step

How to Use

Step 1

Start with clean, dry lips, free of balm or oils.

Step 2

Apply the tint from the bear tube in an even layer over your lips.

Step 3

Let it dry fully, keeping your lips apart while the film sets.

Step 4

Peel the film away from one corner to reveal the stain, then add balm over the top if you like a glossier finish.

Good to Know

Questions & Answers

Why is it called a lip tattoo?

It is a peel-off lip tint: once the dried film is peeled away, the colour is left behind as a stain on the lips — a bit like a temporary tattoo for your lips.

How is it different from a normal lip tint?

Instead of painting colour on and leaving it wet, you apply it, let the film set, then peel the film off — the stain underneath is the finish.
